## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice). One `index.html` plus local assets, deployable to any static host.

## Users

- **User:** maintenance staff, plant engineers and technical operators in industrial SMEs (PyMEs) who supervise machinery day to day.
- **Buyer:** maintenance managers, plant managers and operations managers who want fewer unplanned stops and lower operating costs.
- **Decision maker:** general managers and owners of the PyME, who need a return on investment and operating continuity during electrical failures.
- Initial market: manufacturing and metal-mechanic PyMEs in Nuevo León.

## Product Purpose

VoltAnalytics monitors, analyzes and predicts the electrical behavior of a plant. IoT measurement nodes read voltage, current, active/reactive/apparent power, frequency and power factor in real time; data goes to the cloud, AI time-series models find consumption patterns, forecast demand and flag anomalies; a web dashboard shows indicators, history, alerts and forecasts so the plant acts before a failure, not after the bill.

## Positioning

Brings monitoring + storage + analytics + prediction, normally only available to large industry, to PyMEs with accessible hardware and a modular architecture that grows by adding nodes without replacing the system. Prefers an own tool over continuous external consulting.

## Operating Context

- Adoption path: contact and diagnosis with the maintenance manager → pilot of 30 to 60 days on a critical production line → cost-benefit report → full deployment.
- Install: non-invasive current transformers, measurement module (PZEM-004T), microcontroller with IoT connectivity (Arduino UNO Q), industrial NEMA/IP enclosure, installed by staff following NOM-029-STPS-2011 and NOM-001-SEDE.
- Reference standards: IEEE 519-2022 (harmonics), IEEE 1159-2019 (power quality), NOM-001-SEDE, NOM-029-STPS-2011.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 7 electrical variables monitored; real-time dashboard; historical storage; alerts; consumption forecasting; anomaly detection (e.g. low power factor, overloads, voltage variation).
- Maturity: TRL 5, prototype installed in a laboratory; field pilots are the next step. Do not claim commercial availability or field results.

## Product Principles

1. Prevent, don't react: every feature turns data into a decision before the failure.
2. Accessible to a PyME: plain language for operators, no consulting dependence.
3. Honest maturity: show what the prototype does today, label the pilot as the next step.
4. Grounded in standards: IEEE and NOM references are part of the trust story.
